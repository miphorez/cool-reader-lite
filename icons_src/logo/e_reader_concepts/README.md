# E-reader launcher icon concepts

These files are preview-only and are not wired into Android resources.

- `concept_a.svg`: closest to the supplied sketch.
- `concept_b.svg`: selected compact concept with centered geometry, raised letter, shorter brim, and balanced sepia.
- `concept_c.svg`: stronger reader/person silhouette.
- `concepts_preview.svg`: comparison of shapes and colors on light and dark backgrounds.
- `concept_b_revised_preview.svg`: focused preview of the selected revision.

The reader page base is `#ECE3CB`. The proposed browns are darker, warmer derivatives of that base.
